# Code of Conduct

This project is for everyone. Whatever your experience, background or identity, you are welcome here.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intentions and ask before judging.
- Give and take feedback about the work, never about the person.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who don't follow this code.

If something happens that you want maintainers to know about, contact them privately through GitHub (for example via the repository's security advisory form, or a maintainer's profile). Reports are handled confidentially.
